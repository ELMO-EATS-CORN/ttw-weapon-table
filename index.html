TTW WEAPON TABLE □

Statistics for all 546 weapon records in a Tale of Two Wastelands load order, extracted with FNVEdit — every value is the winning record data the game engine actually uses, not wiki numbers. Critical hit calculator — how crit chance is computed ▼
a% × b = c95%
a — base critical chance (%)
The character’s base critical chance, before the weapon’s multiplier. Luck grants +1% per point; the Finesse perk adds +5; the Built to Destroy trait adds +3; the 1st Recon Beret adds +5; crit-boosting armor such as Ulysses’ Duster or Elite Riot Gear adds +5; each True Police Stories magazine adds +5, or +10 with the Comprehension perk. The Pip-Boy never displays this total — it is the sum of Luck, perks, and equipped gear, added up by hand. A fully stacked base is about 38%. Flat bonuses — Light Touch (+5 in light armor), VATS (+5), and perks such as Laser Commander, Set Lasers for Fun, and Ninja — are added after the multiplication, so they stay outside a.
b — weapon critical multiplier
Each weapon multiplies the base chance by its own critical multiplier — the Crit× column of the table below. Most weapons are ×1; a multiplier of ×2 or more makes a weapon a strong critical-build candidate. The in-game weapon page only shows a qualitative “better critical chance”, so record data like this dump is the exact source.
c — final chance to crit per shot (%)
The chance that any single shot scores a critical hit, capped at 100%. Critical bonus damage is added to the weapon’s base damage before the target’s damage threshold is applied, so it helps push through DT rather than bypassing it. Sneak attacks made while hidden are guaranteed criticals no matter what c says, except with weapons whose Crit× is 0.

Pick a weapon in each dropdown for a stat-by-stat diff; green = better, red = worse.

Raw record fields from FNVEdit. Damage is base weapon damage before ammo modifiers, perks, or difficulty; ammunition adds further crit-damage multipliers (e.g. .308 match, hollow point). “Host file” = which master owns the record in the load order; “Winning file” = which plugin supplies the final values. The Usable column marks creature/robot AI weapons (every leftover “Big Guns” tag in this data belongs to an AI weapon), companion-only copies, Anchorage simulation-only versions (records whose editor ID has a “Wasteland” twin), and cut/test records — the availability filter reveals them. Ammo names are shown in readable form; the raw record value appears as a tooltip on the ammo column, and console commands use FormIDs rather than any of these names.

About this page. Stats come from a dump of a Tale of Two Wastelands 3.4 install (extracted with FNVEdit); values may differ with other TTW versions or mods. The page, table and crit calculator were generated with Mistral AI, then reviewed and edited with Claude (Anthropic). The crit formula was checked against community wiki documentation; individual values were not verified one by one in-game, so treat them as reference, not gospel.
